Resto POS – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Resto POS ("the app") is a restaurant billing and accounts app published by KernalScape ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects and how it is used. Contact: admin@kernalscape.com.

1. Data that stays on your phone

Your business data (items, prices, stock, customers, bills, invoices, accounts, settings and item photos) is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. We do not receive it and we cannot see it. It is not uploaded to any server by the app.

Backups: when you choose "Backup", the app creates a file and you choose where to save it. When you choose "Restore", you pick a file from your phone. We never receive these files.

2. Data we collect

Email address (license unlock). To unlock the app you enter your email address and a 6-digit code we send to it. The email address is sent to our license server and stored there so we can recognise a purchase you have already made, including on a new phone. The server returns a digitally signed license that the app keeps on your phone.

Payment. The one-time unlock is paid through Razorpay. Payment details (card, UPI, bank) are entered in Razorpay's screen and are handled by Razorpay under its own privacy policy. We never see or store your card or UPI details. Our server receives only the payment confirmation (order and payment identifiers) and the email it belongs to.

We do not collect your name, location, contacts, phone number, advertising ID or device identifiers. The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

3. Permissions

  • Internet: only to verify your email and complete the one-time unlock.
  • Bluetooth / nearby devices: to find and print bills on your paired thermal printer. No Bluetooth data leaves the phone.
  • Camera / photos: only if you choose to add a photo to an item. The photo is saved on your phone only.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. Your email address and payment confirmation are processed by: (a) our license server, to issue your license; (b) our email provider, to send you the code; and (c) Razorpay, to take payment. We share data otherwise only if the law requires it.

5. Security

Communication with the license server uses HTTPS. The license is verified on the phone with a digital signature. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the data we hold.

6. Retention and deletion

Data on your phone stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. We keep your email address and purchase record for as long as your license is valid, so that you can restore it. To have your email address and purchase record deleted, email us at admin@kernalscape.com. Note that deleting the record may mean you cannot restore your unlock later.

7. Children

The app is intended for restaurant owners and staff. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
