Resto POS – Delete your account and data

App: Resto POS · Developer: KernalScape · Last updated: 26 November 2026

Your Resto POS license is linked to the email address you verified when you unlocked the app. You can ask us to delete that account and the data we hold about it.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to admin@kernalscape.com with the subject "Delete my Resto POS account".
  2. Send it from the same email address you used to unlock the app, so we can confirm it is yours.
  3. We will reply to confirm, and delete the data listed below within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your email address and license record on our license server.
  • Any verification codes linked to your email address.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Payment records (payment and order identifiers, amount and date) are kept for up to 7 years where we are required to keep them for tax and accounting purposes. They are not used for any other purpose.
  • Payment details such as card or UPI numbers are held by Razorpay, not by us. See Razorpay's privacy policy to ask it to remove them.

Data on your phone

Your business data (items, bills, stock, accounts and photos) is stored only on your phone, and we do not have it. To remove it, clear the app's data in your phone's Settings or uninstall the app. Backup files you saved yourself must be deleted by you.

After deletion

Deleting your record means we can no longer restore your unlock on a new phone, and you may need to pay again to unlock the app.

Questions? Write to admin@kernalscape.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
